The Hatch from Mill Lane in the 19th century - looking north from the bridge by the mill.

The building on the extreme left was the granary (see below) and still exists as the offices of Shortfield Garage. The other buildings on the left were replaced by industrial buildings in the 1870s.

In the distance is The Hatch and it can be seen that the front of it was at right-angles to the lane. The bow window under the gable still exists
