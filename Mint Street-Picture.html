In the 1540s, this street was called Sheet Street (possibly derived from the old English "sceatt" - pronounced "sheet" - meaning money). Later it was referred to as Owten Lane (after Owtens, the old name for Brook House). By 1653, it was as 'Harts Lane, otherwise known as Owtens Lane' (Source: Woods H5p372)
Since about 1900, it has been Mint Street.
