 This shows the eastern end of Godalming from South Hill at about 1895, The smoke rising in the centre is from a train in the Old Station, which stood on the corner of Chalk Road and Meadrow  

Postcard: GIOPP vol 2, no. 2

image: Charterhouse Archives/Miscellaneous
This was the view looking down Holloway Hill, c1896.

Postcard: GIOPP vol 1, no. 58
This view shows the gas works, which stood where the police station is now

The railway embankment and the Lammas Lands are in the background

  

Postcard: GIOPP vol 1, no.19
This view shows the town from New Way. The chimney of the tannery belches smoke. Neither of the chimneys has survived and the mill pond in the foreground is now a car park
