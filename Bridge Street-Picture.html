This gets its name because it leads to the Town Bridge, but it has previously been known as The Stream, Water Lane and Watery Lane because of the flow of water down it. This water is said to have flowed down Brighton Road and into Bridge Street in times of heavy rain.

A similar view some years earlier. The buildings on the right were built by George Marshall in about 1835 and were demolished when The Burys was created, in about 1961.

On the left areThe West Surrey House (then a brewery) and Up to date Boots. On the right is the Horse & Groom, which at one time housed the public library.
